iPod Transcode & Folder Sync

Compare a source music folder against a target (iPod) folder. Missing child folders (albums) can be selected and synced.

FLAC files are resampled to 44.1kHz and/or reduced to 16-bit when needed to best fit the 4th gen iPod I own. Other formats are copied nearly verbatim over, with the hope that the iPod can play it.

Embedded cover art in any format is scaled down to 128px, so will a sidecar image.

Everything runs in the browser, no server needed.
